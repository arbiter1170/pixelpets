# Fernbrook Heights level ladder

Anchors from the live teams. Route 1 is one Walkling at a time: Pia gust 4 then beetle 5, Tam crab 5 then crab 6, Ollie moth 6 then dormouse 7. Tobin is beetle 8, then moth 9. Fen is mole 12, beetle 13, and mole stage-1 14, with Thorn Lash and one Heal Snack. A partner who only fought Tobin arrives at Lv 7 (battle XP 163). One who also beat Pia, Tam, and Ollie arrives at Lv 9 (297). Starters still have a 35-power move until Lv 12.

In a group fight the partner moves first and picks a target. Then every foe still standing attacks. Two Lv 6 hits into an Ember are about 28 damage. Tobin's moth at Lv 9 lands about 19, once. Fen's Thorn Lash is about 21 from the Lv 12 mole and about 29 from the stage-1 Lv 14, across three bodies. A Lv 8 pair that already knows a same-type move (learned at Lv 5) faints a Lv 7–8 partner inside two Heal Snacks. The drafted 8/8, 9/9, 10/10, and 11/11 teams do not clear.

Numbers below use `damage()` and `defaultMoves`, stage 0, a low damage roll, foes on their best hit, and no crits. Basic AI picks that hit about 70% of the time, so a live fight is a little kinder. Evolution is not required. The ally auto-attack is Bump (power 30). One path per run.

## Snacks

On a Lv 7 arrival, a careful Ember spends 4 Heal Snacks: none on the door, 2 on each Lv 5 pair, and on the tunnels 2 more on Mara (Quinn itself is free). Tide spends 1 on the evil door and 0 on the tunnels. Bloom spends 1, or 2 if the evil door rolls low. A partner who arrived at Lv 9 spends 1 snack on the whole evil path and 0 on the tunnels.

The free 2 cover the door. The Lv 5 fight wants two more, at 50 Acorns each. Tobin pays 90. Pia, Tam, Ollie, and Tobin together pay 270. A Hearty Snack is a spare. Leave the Wake Tonic: it costs 400 and wakes a friend at 25% HP into two more hits. Floor pickups (Nia, the lab floors, Jed, the tunnel) are the other two snacks, and the ridge shop is there if a pickup is missed.

## Soft-lock

The free snacks are a one-shot grass pickup at (10, 12), off the south path. Kit's stock-up scene does not hand them over. A Lv 7 Bloom on a low roll needs one or both for the door. With only Tobin's 90 Acorns they can buy one Heal Snack. Grant the two snacks inside that stock-up scene, and keep the crate shop on the ridge in front of both doors.

The retry also needs a Lantern rest on the wake. A wipe that leaves the only partner napping for 20 minutes cannot be fought again. The Wake Tonic that would cut that nap short is not in the bag yet.

## Against Tobin and Fen

At Lv 9 the Lv 6 finale is a win, with Ember finishing around 1 HP. At Lv 10 Ember still finishes it around 7 HP, and finishes a replay of Tobin around 20 HP with no snack. Tide is still stopped by Tobin's moth at both levels. Fen's three Thorn Lashes, with her one Heal Snack, faint all three starters at Lv 9 and at Lv 10. The last Heights fight is the harder round. The trial is still the wall.
